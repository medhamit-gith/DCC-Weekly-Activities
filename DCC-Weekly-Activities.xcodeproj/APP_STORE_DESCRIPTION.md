# Privacy Policy for DCC Weekly Activities

**Last Updated: February 13, 2026**

## Introduction

DCC Weekly Activities ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### Strava Account Data
When you sign in with Strava, we request access to:
- Your public profile information (name, profile photo)
- Your club membership information
- Weekly cycling activities from your club (distance, elevation, duration)
- Activity metadata (date, type, public visibility)

### Biometric Data (Optional)
- If you enable Face ID or Touch ID, biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device
- We never receive, store, or transmit your biometric information
- This feature is optional and can be disabled at any time

### Device Information
- Device type and iOS version (for compatibility)
- App crashes and performance data (only if you opt-in)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Display weekly club statistics and leaderboards
- Show your personal cycling achievements
- Provide data visualizations and insights
- Improve app performance and user experience

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All Strava data is cached locally on your device
- Authentication tokens are stored securely in iOS Keychain
- Cache is automatically cleared after 7 days

### No Cloud Storage
- We do NOT store your data on external servers
- We do NOT share your data with third parties
- We do NOT sell your information to advertisers

### Security Measures
- Secure HTTPS connections for all API requests
- iOS Keychain for sensitive token storage
- Optional biometric authentication
- Automatic token expiration and refresh

## Third-Party Services

### Strava API
- We use Strava's official API to fetch club data
- Subject to Strava's Privacy Policy: https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy
- We only request READ permissions
- We never modify or delete your Strava activities

### No Analytics or Tracking
- We do NOT use analytics services
- We do NOT track your behavior
- We do NOT collect identifiable usage data

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Deletion
- View your data anytime within the app
- Sign out to immediately stop data access
- Uninstall the app to delete all local data
- Revoke Strava permissions at: https://www.strava.com/settings/apps

### Opt-Out Options
- Biometric authentication: Disable in app settings
- Data caching: Clear cache in app settings
- All features: Sign out or uninstall the app

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in:
- App features and functionality
- Legal requirements
- User feedback and best practices

You'll be notified of significant changes through:
- App update notes
- In-app notification
- Email (if provided)

- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

## Consent
