❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to the app?
The app uses Strava authentication. Simply tap "Login with Strava" and authorize the app to access your Desi Cycling Club data. You must be a member of the Desi Cycling Club on Strava.
Why can't I see any activities?
Make sure you:
  • Are logged in with Strava
  • Are a member of the Desi Cycling Club on Strava
  • Have a stable internet connection
  • Have granted the app permission to read your activities
If you still don't see activities, try logging out and logging back in.
How do I revoke app access?
You can revoke access at any time by:
  • Going to Strava Settings → My Apps
  • Finding "DCC Weekly Activities"
  • Clicking "Revoke Access"
Or simply log out within the app.
Is my data safe?
Yes! All data is stored locally on your device. We don't operate any servers and don't have access to your data. Authentication tokens are stored securely in iOS Keychain. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I enable Face ID / Touch ID?
Go to the app settings and enable "Biometric Authentication". This feature is optional and adds an extra layer of security when opening the app.
Why aren't my activities showing up on the leaderboard?
The leaderboard shows activities for the current week (Monday to Sunday). Make sure:
  • Your activities are posted to Strava
  • You're a member of the Desi Cycling Club
  • Your Strava privacy settings allow club visibility
  • The activity was completed in the current week
The app says "Too many requests" - what does this mean?
Strava has rate limits on API requests. If you see this message, please wait 15 minutes before trying again. The app automatically manages rate limits, but refreshing too frequently can trigger this.
Can I use this app if I'm not in the Desi Cycling Club?
No, this app is specifically designed for Desi Cycling Club members. You must join the club on Strava to use the app. Visit the DCC Strava page to join.
How do I delete my data?
Simply:
  • Log out of the app (this removes authentication tokens)
  • Delete the app (this removes all cached data)
All data is stored locally on your device, so deleting the app removes everything.

🚴‍♂️ About the App

DCC Weekly Activities is an unofficial app created for the Desi Cycling Club community. It helps members track weekly activities, view leaderboards, and stay motivated.

This app is not affiliated with Strava, Inc. All activity data is retrieved from Strava using their official API.

📱 System Requirements

  • iOS 15.0 or later
  • Active Strava account
  • Membership in the Desi Cycling Club on Strava
  • Internet connection for data sync
