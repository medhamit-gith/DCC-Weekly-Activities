## DESCRIPTION
(4000 characters maximum)

**Your cycling club's weekly performance, visualized.**

DCC Weekly Activities connects to your Strava account and transforms your club's ride data into beautiful, easy-to-understand insights. See who rode the most this week, how you compare to the top performers, and discover detailed breakdowns of every member's activities—all in one clean, fast interface.

**Core Features:**

• **Connect Securely** — One-tap Strava OAuth login with Face ID or Touch ID protection  
• **Weekly Club Summary** — See total distance, rides, elevation, and active members at a glance  
• **Interactive Charts** — Tap any stat to explore three detailed analysis modes  
• **Just My Stats** — Your complete weekly breakdown with club average comparisons  
• **Me vs Top 3** — Head-to-head performance charts comparing you to the distance leaders  
• **Worst Performer Analysis** — Discover who had the quietest week and why (with respectful, data-driven insights)  
• **Member Profiles** — Tap any rider to see their full activity history and performance trends  
• **Activity Details** — View individual ride metrics including distance, elevation, speed, power, and heart rate  
• **Beautiful Visualizations** — Built with Swift Charts for smooth, native performance  
• **Smart Date Ranges** — Automatically extends to 2 weeks when the current week has no data  

**Privacy and Data:**

Your privacy is our priority. DCC Weekly Activities operates with complete transparency:

• **Read-Only Access** — We never write to, modify, or delete any Strava activity or user data  
• **No Cloud Storage** — All data stays on your device; we don't operate any servers that store your information  
• **No Tracking** — Zero analytics, zero advertising, zero data collection beyond what's needed to display your stats  
• **Secure Token Storage** — Your Strava access credentials are encrypted in iOS Keychain and never leave your device  
• **In-Memory Processing** — Activity data is loaded on demand and cleared when you close the app  

This app uses the Strava API strictly in accordance with Strava's API Agreement and Brand Guidelines. We display publicly available club activity data that's already visible on Strava's own platform—just in a more focused, club-centric format.

**Strava Account Required:**

A Strava account is required to use this app. When you connect, we request the following permissions:

• **read** — View your basic Strava profile (name and location)  
• **activity:read** — View your club's activity feed (distance, elevation, speed, time, power, heart rate)  

These are read-only permissions. The app cannot post activities, modify existing rides, or access private data beyond what your club already shares.

**Who This App Is For:**

Designed specifically for members of cycling clubs who want to stay motivated, track progress, and celebrate achievements together. Whether you're chasing the leaderboard or just keeping tabs on your weekly consistency, DCC Weekly Activities gives you the insights you need in seconds.
