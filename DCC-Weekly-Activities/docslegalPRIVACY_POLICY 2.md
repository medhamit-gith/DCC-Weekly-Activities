## What Data We Collect

### 1. Strava OAuth Access Token
- **What it is**: A secure credential that allows the app to read your Strava data on your behalf
- **How we get it**: Generated by Strava when you tap "Connect with Strava" and authorize the app
- **Where it's stored**: iOS Keychain on your device only (encrypted with AES-256, protected by your device passcode/biometric)
- **Who has access**: Only you, on your device. Not transmitted to any server we operate.

### 2. Strava OAuth Refresh Token
- **What it is**: A long-lived credential used to obtain new access tokens when the current one expires
- **How we get it**: Provided by Strava during the initial OAuth authorization
- **Where it's stored**: iOS Keychain on your device only (same encryption as access token)
- **Who has access**: Only you, on your device

### 3. Your Athlete Profile
- **What we collect**:
  - First name
  - Last name
  - Profile photo URL (optional - only if set on Strava)
  - City, state, country (optional - only if set on Strava)
- **How we get it**: Fetched from Strava's `/api/v3/athlete` endpoint using your access token
- **How it's used**: To personalize your dashboard greeting (e.g., "Hi, John! 👋")
- **Where it's stored**: In-memory (RAM) only while the app is open. Cleared when you close the app.
- **Who has access**: Only you, on your device

### 4. Club Activity Data
- **What we collect**:
  - Athlete names (from club members whose activities appear in the club feed)
  - Activity names (e.g., "Morning Ride")
  - Distance (kilometers)
  - Elevation gain (meters)
  - Moving time (hours/minutes)
  - Average speed (km/h)
  - Power data (watts) - only if available in the Strava activity
  - Heart rate data - only if available in the Strava activity
  - Suffer score - only if available in the Strava activity
- **How we get it**: Fetched from Strava's `/api/v3/clubs/212760/activities` endpoint using your access token
- **How it's used**: To calculate and display weekly performance statistics, charts, and comparisons
- **Where it's stored**: In-memory (RAM) only while the app is open. Cleared when you close the app.
- **Who has access**: Only you, on your device. This data is **already publicly visible** to all members of your Strava club.

## Third-Party Services

### Strava API
- **Service**: Strava, Inc.
- **Purpose**: Fetch athlete profile and club activity data
- **Privacy Policy**: https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy
- **Data shared**: Your OAuth access token (in the Authorization header)
- **What we access**: 
  - Read-only access to your athlete profile
  - Read-only access to your club's public activity feed
- **What we do NOT access**:
  - Cannot post, modify, or delete your Strava activities
  - Cannot access your private activities (only club-visible activities)
  - Cannot send messages or kudos on your behalf
  - Cannot follow/unfollow athletes on your behalf

**OAuth Scopes Requested**:
- `read` - View your basic profile (name, location)
- `activity:read` - View club activity data (distances, times, speeds)

Both scopes are **read-only**. The app has no write or modify permissions.

### Cloudflare Worker (Token Exchange Only)
- **Service**: Cloudflare Workers (serverless function)
- **URL**: `https://dcc-strava.amit-r-kamat.workers.dev`
- **Purpose**: Securely exchange OAuth authorization codes for access tokens without exposing Strava's client secret in the app binary
- **What it does**:
  1. Receives authorization code from the app
  2. Sends a POST request to Strava's `/oauth/token` endpoint with the code + client secret
  3. Returns the access token to the app
  4. **Does NOT log, store, or retain any data**
- **Why it's needed**: OAuth 2.0 requires a client secret to exchange codes for tokens. Hardcoding the secret in the app would expose it to reverse engineering. The Cloudflare Worker acts as a secure proxy.
- **Privacy**: This is a **stateless function** that processes the exchange and immediately discards all data. No user information is retained.

**Developer Note**: If you require verification of the Cloudflare Worker's source code, it can be provided upon request.

## Your Rights and Choices

### Revoke Strava Access
You can revoke the app's access to your Strava data at any time:

1. Go to https://www.strava.com/settings/apps
2. Find "DCC Weekly Activities" in the authorized apps list
3. Click "Revoke Access"

**Effect**: The app will no longer be able to fetch your profile or club data. Next time you open the app, you'll be prompted to reconnect.

### Delete Your Local Data
To delete all data stored on your device:

**Option 1** (Recommended): Delete the app
1. Long-press the app icon on your home screen
2. Tap "Remove App" → "Delete App"

**Effect**: All Keychain data (tokens), all cached data, and all app files are permanently deleted.

**Option 2**: Log out within the app
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Log Out" (if available in settings or menu)

**Effect**: Access token and refresh token are deleted from Keychain. You'll return to the login screen.

### Data Portability
Since all data displayed in the app comes directly from Strava, you already have access to it via Strava's own export tools:

- Strava account data export: https://www.strava.com/athlete/delete_your_account

The app itself does not store any data beyond what's already on Strava.

### Do Not Sell My Personal Information (CCPA)
**We do not sell your personal information.** We do not collect personal information for the purpose of selling it. This app is provided free of charge with no monetization.

### GDPR Rights (European Users)
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right to access**: You can access your data by viewing it within the app or on Strava
- **Right to rectification**: Update your data on Strava; the app will reflect changes on next refresh
- **Right to erasure**: Delete the app and/or revoke Strava access (see above)
- **Right to data portability**: Export your data from Strava directly
- **Right to object**: Revoke Strava access or stop using the app

## Data Security

We take security seriously, even though we don't operate servers that store your data.

### On Your Device
- **Keychain encryption**: AES-256 (industry standard)
- **Biometric protection**: Face ID or Touch ID required to unlock the app (if enabled)
- **No backup to iCloud**: Keychain items are flagged `ThisDeviceOnly`
- **No screenshots in App Switcher**: Sensitive screens can be hidden (if implemented)

### In Transit
- **HTTPS only**: All API requests use TLS 1.2 or higher
- **Certificate pinning**: (Not implemented in v1.0, consider for future versions)

### What You Should Do
