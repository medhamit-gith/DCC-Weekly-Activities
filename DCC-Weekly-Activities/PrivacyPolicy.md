# Privacy Policy for DCC Weekly Activities

**Last Updated: February 13, 2026**

## Introduction

DCC Weekly Activities ("the App") is designed to help members of the Desi Cycling Club view and track club activities from Strava. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Strava Account Information
When you connect your Strava account to the App, we collect:
- Your Strava club activities data
- Activity names, distances, dates, speeds, and elevation
- Your Strava member name
- Activity statistics

### Authentication Data
- Strava OAuth access tokens (stored securely in device Keychain)
- Biometric authentication data (Face ID/Touch ID - processed locally, never transmitted)

### Technical Information
- Device type and iOS version (for compatibility)
- App usage statistics (crashes, errors)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
1. **Display Club Statistics**: Show weekly activity summaries and member rankings
2. **Authenticate Users**: Securely connect your Strava account
3. **Improve the App**: Fix bugs and enhance features

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only
- All data is stored **locally on your device**
- We do **NOT** store your data on external servers
- We do **NOT** transmit your data to third parties (except Strava API)

### Security Measures
- Strava access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain (encrypted)
- Biometric authentication (Face ID/Touch ID) protects app access
- All API communications use HTTPS encryption
- No sensitive data is logged or transmitted

## Third-Party Services

### Strava API
We use the Strava API to fetch club activity data. When you connect your account:
- You authorize the App to read your club activities
- Strava's Privacy Policy applies to their services: https://www.strava.com/legal/privacy
- We comply with Strava's API Terms of Service

### No Other Third Parties
- We do **NOT** use analytics services
- We do **NOT** use advertising networks
- We do **NOT** sell your data to anyone

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Automatic Deletion
- Activity data is refreshed weekly and old data is automatically removed
- Cached data is stored temporarily and cleared regularly

### Manual Deletion
You can delete all your data by:
1. Tapping "Log Out" in the app
2. Revoking app access in your Strava settings
3. Deleting the app from your device

All locally stored data will be permanently deleted.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: View all data we collect about you (through the app interface)
- **Delete**: Remove your data at any time (log out or delete app)
- **Revoke Access**: Disconnect Strava authorization in your Strava account settings
- **Data Portability**: Export your Strava data directly from Strava

## Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Displaying a notice in the app (for significant changes)
- Posting the new policy on this page

## International Users

The app is designed for members of the Desi Cycling Club and may be used internationally. Data is processed on your local device and transmitted only to Strava's API (which may be hosted globally).

## Legal Compliance

We comply with:
- Apple's App Store Review Guidelines
- Strava's API Terms of Service
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable
