## Description (4000 characters max)

**Join the Desi Cycling Club community and track your weekly riding achievements!**

DCC Weekly Activities is the perfect companion app for members of the Desi Cycling Club. Stay motivated, track your progress, and celebrate your cycling achievements with fellow club members.

**KEY FEATURES**

📊 COMPREHENSIVE STATISTICS
• View weekly club activity summaries
• Track total distance, rides, elevation, and speed
• Compare your performance with other members
• Beautiful charts and visualizations
• Top performers leaderboard

🏆 MEMBER RANKINGS
• See how you rank among club members
• Track performance trends week over week
• Celebrate top performers and personal bests
• Friendly competition to keep you motivated

🎨 BEAUTIFUL VISUALIZATIONS
• Interactive bar charts and pie charts
• Color-coded performance trends
• Easy-to-read tables and lists
• Smooth animations and transitions
• Indian flag-inspired design theme

🔒 SECURE & PRIVATE
• Protected with Face ID or Touch ID
• Your Strava data stays on your device
• Secure authentication with Strava
• No data sold to third parties
• Privacy-first design

📱 DESIGNED FOR ALL DEVICES
• Optimized for iPhone and iPad
• Apple TV support for big-screen viewing
• Beautiful on all screen sizes
• Native iOS design

**HOW IT WORKS**

1. Connect your Strava account securely
2. Automatically fetch your club's weekly activities
3. View comprehensive statistics and rankings
4. Track your progress and celebrate achievements
5. Stay motivated with friendly competition

**PERFECT FOR**

• Members of the Desi Cycling Club
• Cyclists who love tracking statistics
• Anyone looking to stay motivated
• Competitive riders who enjoy leaderboards
• Casual riders who want to see club activity

**STRAVA INTEGRATION**

This app integrates seamlessly with Strava to fetch club activity data. You'll need:
• A Strava account
• Membership in the Desi Cycling Club on Strava
• Permission to access your club activities
